Design
Principles

Six rules that define the brutalist-paper visual language.

01
Hard Shadows Only
No blur, no gradient. Every shadow is a solid offset block — 4px/4px for cards, 10px/10px for hero blocks.
02
Raw Paper Canvas
#F4EFE7 unbleached paper base. No subtle gradients, no glass morphism — texture comes from typography.
03
Serif + Sans + Mono
Georgia for editorial weight. Inter for body and interface. SF Mono for code, labels, and metadata.
04
Zero Radius
No rounded corners anywhere. The rectangle is the most honest shape. Sharp edges convey precision.
05
Terracotta & Gold
#B84F35 rust accent against #D89A57 warm gold. Industrial palette — brick, iron, paper, ink.
06
Declare, Don't Decorate
No icons without information. Borders are structural, not ornamental. Every element earns its place.
