Context & Brief05 / 11

The Question We Walked In With

Every project at Boring Studios begins with a single sentence that the team argues about for at least a week. This year it was about light.

  • How does a public square hold light across a season?
  • What changes when you design for the hour after sunset instead of the hour before?
  • Can a room be tuned to make people read slower?
  • Is there a color that means "stay a while" in any language?
  • What happens when you treat a museum like a weather station?

Research Findings06 / 11

Six Weeks Across Three Sites

We embedded researchers in three public buildings — a library in Rotterdam, a market hall in Antwerp, and a former post office in Ghent — for six weeks each.

  • 87% of visitors changed their path based on window light alone.
  • People read 2.3× longer when lit by a single warm source vs. overhead grid.
  • The most-used seat in each building was never the one closest to the door.
  • Staff reported fewer complaints on days with variable cloud cover than on fully sunny or fully overcast days.
  • Three out of four returning visitors came back at the same time of day.

Design Principles10 / 11

What We Kept

  • A single typeface at a single weight carries the house voice.
  • Yellow means "pay attention"; near-black means "settle in." No third meaning.
  • Every surface is flat. Depth comes from contrast, not elevation.
  • The headline is always the loudest element on the slide. Always.

What We Dropped

  • Rounded corners — every corner in the system is now a strict rectangle.
  • Gradients — surfaces are binary; transition happens at the hairline, not in a blend.
  • Italic and underline — the only emphasis mechanism is weight contrast: 900 vs 400.
  • Third accent colors — if it's not yellow or near-black, it doesn't belong here.

Design System12 / 14

设计原则

01 / 字体即图形体量
Barlow 900 全大写展示尺度——字体不再是字体，而是形状。
02 / 二元 + 一
近黑底，酸黄字。或酸黄底，近黑字。不存在第三种颜色。
03 / 平面且严苛
圆角归零，投影归零，渐变归零。深度来自对比与字重，不来自层级。
04 / 等宽即元数据
IBM Plex Mono 仅用于 chrome、计数器、标签。其余全用 Barlow。
05 / 极细线 + 2px 锚点
1px chrome 边框，2px 统计顶边与图表基线。仅实线，不用虚线。
06 / 破折号引导
每个列表项前缀用 —— 以强调色标出。永不用圆点。
